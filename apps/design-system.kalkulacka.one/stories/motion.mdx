# Motion

- **durations** — `fast`, `base` and `slow`; used as `ko:duration-fast`, …; a theme pins them with `--ko-motion-duration-fast`, …. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` all three collapse to effectively zero.
- **easings** — `spring` for entrances and presses, `exit` for leaving elements; used as `ko:ease-spring`, `ko:ease-exit`; pinned with `--ko-motion-easing-spring`, ….

## Pressable scales

The transform scales every pressable component shares, consumed as `var(…)`: `--ko-pressable-scale-hover` (a whisper larger), `-press` (a touch smaller), `-open` and `-open-hover` (an opened card, and it hovered). Current values live in `styles.css`.
